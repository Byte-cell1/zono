Quick answers

Is Zono really free?

Yes. You can play every game in your browser without paying or downloading anything.

How do I play with friends?

Create a private room, copy the room code and send it to your friend. They enter the code and you start playing.

Do I need an account?

An account saves your XP, achievements and leaderboard rank, and lets you enter tournaments.

Does it work on my phone?

Zono runs in any modern mobile or desktop browser, so there is nothing to install.
